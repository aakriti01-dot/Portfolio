01 · About

A little about me

I’m a computer science student and developer who enjoys turning ideas into things I can actually build and use. I’m particularly interested in web development and in understanding how the different parts of an application come together from what a user sees and interacts with to what happens behind the scenes.

Most of my learning has come through building. I enjoy taking something I’ve learned through coursework or an idea I have and turning it into a working project. Along the way, I’ve learned that development is as much about solving problems and understanding why something works as it is about writing code.

I’m always looking for opportunities to learn, experiment, and turn what I’m learning into something useful. As I continue through university, I want to keep strengthening my skills, take on more meaningful projects, and create work that reflects both my technical growth and the way I think.
